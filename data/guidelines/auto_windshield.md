# Auto Windshield Replacement Guideline

**Guideline ID:** GL-001  
**Category:** Auto / Comprehensive  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## Coverage Rule

Windshield replacement or repair is covered under comprehensive auto coverage when:
- Damage is caused by road debris, hail, or vandalism.
- Policy is active and premium is current.
- Claim amount does not exceed $1,500 for full replacement.

## Deductible Waiver

Many states require deductible waivers for windshield claims. If the policy holder is in a mandated state, the deductible is waived.

## Approval Threshold

Claims up to $1,500 for windshield repair/replacement may be **auto-approved** provided coverage is confirmed.

Claims above $1,500 require photographic evidence and adjuster sign-off.

## Denial Conditions

- Policy not active at the time of loss.
- Peril not covered (e.g., intentional damage by the insured).
- Prior windshield claim within the last 12 months (fraud indicator).
