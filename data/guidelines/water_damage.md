# Water Damage Exclusions Guideline

**Guideline ID:** GL-005  
**Category:** Homeowners / Property  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## Covered Water Damage

The following water damage scenarios are covered under standard homeowners policies with water damage coverage:

- **Sudden and accidental discharge**: e.g., burst pipes, overflowing washing machine.
- **Storm-related intrusion**: rain entering through storm-damaged roof or windows.
- **Appliance failure**: sudden failure of water heater, dishwasher, or refrigerator.

## Excluded Water Damage

The following are **explicitly excluded** and must be **DENIED**:

1. **Flood damage**: Rising water from external sources (rivers, storm surge) is NOT covered. Policyholders must carry separate flood insurance (NFIP or private).
2. **Gradual leaks**: Damage from slow, long-term leaks (e.g., dripping pipe over months) is excluded. The insured is expected to maintain the property.
3. **Sewer or drain backup**: Unless the policy includes a specific sewer backup rider.
4. **Negligence**: Damage caused by failure to maintain the property (e.g., known roof issue left unrepaired).

## Decision Guide

| Scenario | Decision |
|---|---|
| Burst pipe, sudden | APPROVE (if coverage active) |
| Flood from street | DENY (refer to NFIP) |
| Slow leak discovered | DENY (gradual damage excluded) |
| Storm roof + rain damage | APPROVE (if storm rider present) |
| Sewer backup, no rider | DENY |

## Amount Threshold

Water damage claims above $5,000 must follow the high-value review guideline (GL-003).
