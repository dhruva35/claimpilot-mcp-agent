# High-Value Claim Review Guideline

**Guideline ID:** GL-003  
**Category:** Claim Triage / Risk Management  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## Threshold

Any claim with a requested payout exceeding **$5,000 USD** requires mandatory human adjuster review before a decision is recorded.

## Rationale

High-value claims carry elevated risk of:
- Fraud or staged incidents.
- Coverage disputes that may require legal review.
- Reinsurance implications.

## Agent Behaviour

Automated systems (AI agents) **must not approve or deny** claims above $5,000. The only permitted action is **ESCALATE** with a summary of findings.

The escalation package must include:
1. Policy coverage confirmation.
2. Claim history summary.
3. Relevant guideline excerpts.
4. Any fraud indicators identified.

## Human Adjuster SLA

Escalated high-value claims must be reviewed within 2 business days.
