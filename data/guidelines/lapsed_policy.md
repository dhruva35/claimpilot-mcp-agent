# Lapsed Policy Claims Guideline

**Guideline ID:** GL-002  
**Category:** Policy Administration  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## Core Rule

**Claims submitted for a loss that occurred after the policy lapse date must be DENIED.**

A policy lapses when:
- Premium payment is not received by the grace period end date (typically 30 days after due date).
- The policy holder voluntarily cancels the policy.
- Underwriting finds material misrepresentation.

## Grace Period

A 30-day grace period applies from the premium due date. During this period, the policy is technically active. Claims that occur **during** the grace period may be honored if payment is subsequently received within 10 business days of the claim.

## Decision Rule

- Loss date BEFORE lapse date → evaluate normally.
- Loss date DURING grace period → conditional approval, flag for follow-up payment.
- Loss date AFTER lapse date → **DENY immediately**, no coverage.

## Documentation Required

- Policy cancellation notice
- Last premium payment receipt
- Date of loss confirmation
