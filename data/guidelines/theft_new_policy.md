# Theft Claims – New Policy Waiting Period Guideline

**Guideline ID:** GL-004  
**Category:** Theft / Fraud Prevention  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## The 60-Day Rule

Theft claims filed within **60 days of policy inception** (the start date of the policy) are classified as **high-risk** and must be escalated for human review.

## Rationale

Historical claims data shows a statistically significant elevation in fraudulent theft claims filed shortly after policy inception. This pattern is known as the "early-claim fraud indicator."

## Decision Rule

- Policy start date to loss date gap < 60 days AND peril is theft → **ESCALATE**
- Policy start date to loss date gap ≥ 60 days AND peril is theft → evaluate normally

## Supporting Investigation Steps

For escalated theft claims:
1. Verify police report was filed within 24 hours of discovery.
2. Cross-reference prior policies for similar claims.
3. Check for rapid upgrade in coverage value near policy inception.
